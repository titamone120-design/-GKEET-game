## App name
GKEET: Shadow Arena

## Short description
Fight 20 rivals in a 3D arena with punches, kicks, blocks and special moves.

## Full description
Enter the arena and fight your way through 20 progressively tougher rivals in GKEET: Shadow Arena.

Move, jump, punch, kick, block and dash. Build your special energy, chain hits into combos, and learn each opponent's timing as the stages become harder.

### Features
- 20 stages with progressively stronger rivals
- 3D humanoid fighters with skeletal movement and combat animations
- Touch controls designed for Android phones
- Punches, kicks, guarding, dashing and special attacks
- Health bars, combo counter and special-energy meter
- Adjustable difficulty and sound settings
- Offline gameplay with progress saved on your device

No account is required. Core gameplay works offline.
